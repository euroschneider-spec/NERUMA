# Payments with Stripe

## Idea
Organisations pay to publish. Registering, building a guide and previewing it stay free; publishing needs a plan. An owner chooses a
plan on a *Plan & billing* page and pays on Stripe's own page (card or SEPA direct debit). Stripe takes the payment every period and
tells NERUMA; NERUMA unlocks or locks publishing from what Stripe says. If payment stops, the organisation has 30 days to fix it; after
that its guides show "This guide isn't available right now" until it pays again. Nothing is deleted, and the address and QR codes stay.

## Why
NERUMA needs an income, and the payment provider is chosen (`DECISIONS.md`, "Payments go through Stripe"). TECH-TASKS §8 has the flow.

## Not in scope
- Paying by invoice and bank transfer (owner, 2026-10-18: later, when institutions ask).
- German e-invoices (XRechnung or ZUGFeRD); check whether Stripe's invoices are enough before the first German customer.
- A list of who has paid in the NERUMA administration: Stripe's dashboard shows it.
- Cancelling the Stripe subscription automatically when an organisation is erased: NERUMA can't, so erasing is refused until it is cancelled in Stripe by hand (owner, 2026-10-18; `PAYMENTS.md`).
- Several plans or prices in the app. One Stripe price; the amount and the period are set in Stripe and can change there.
- Taking real money: waits for the email provider and the lawyer-checked terms and privacy text (TECH-TASKS, suggested order).

## Decisions
- **One plan per organisation** unlocks publishing for all its locations (owner, 2026-10-18).
- **30 days of grace, then hidden** (owner, 2026-10-18). The 30 days start when Stripe first reports the payment as not made (`past_due`,
  `unpaid`, `canceled`, `paused`). A cancelled plan gets the same 30 days after it ends.
- **The webhook writes through one narrow database function with a secret** (owner, 2026-10-18), not with Supabase's all-powerful key.
  `billing_record_subscription` can only set one organisation's plan, and only with the secret whose SHA-256 the database holds.
- **Card and SEPA direct debit first**, through Stripe Checkout; invoice and bank transfer later (owner, 2026-10-18).
- **Payments are off until a date is set** (`private.billing_policy.required_from`, like the two-factor deadline). Until then publishing
  stays free; afterwards an organisation without a plan can't publish, and its guides go offline from that date. Set the date at least 30
  days after telling organisations.
- **No Stripe library.** Two calls (Checkout and the customer page) and the signature check are a few lines with `fetch` and Node's
  `crypto`; this keeps the code that knows about Stripe in one file, so another provider replaces one file (owner asked how hard a switch is).
- **Stripe Tax** works out VAT and asks businesses for their VAT number. It has to be switched on in Stripe's dashboard (`PAYMENTS.md`).
- **Our own table holds the plan** (`organization_subscriptions`). Publishing, hiding and the pages read it; nothing asks Stripe while a
  visitor waits.

copy, because Stripe's events can arrive out of order and two in the same second can't be ordered.
