# The new logo on the site

## Idea
The owner drew a logo (a mark of two shapes, the name "Neruma", and the tagline "Know before you go"). It becomes a vector and replaces the old
text-and-arches brand in the header, the footer and the organisation sidebar, and the browser icon.

## Why
The site still showed a placeholder (three arches and the word NERUMA). The owner asked to see the real logo on the website (2026-10-18).

## Done when
- [x] The header, the footer and the sidebar show the new logo, in the dark, the light and the high-contrast theme, on a phone as well.
- [x] The folded sidebar shows only the mark.
- [x] The link still says "NERUMA" to a screen reader, and keeps working as the way home.
- [x] The browser tab shows the mark (`public/favicon.svg`).
- [x] The six vector files are in `public/brand/` for later use (emails, social images, print).

## Not in scope
- The tagline inside the logo: it is English only and the site is in three languages, so the site uses the compact logo (mark and name) and keeps writing
  its tagline as text in each language.
- The small "NERUMA" label at the top of the public guide (`guest-guide.tsx`): it is text in the guide's own header, not the logo.
- PNG versions for emails and social previews (needs a raster export; later, with the email templates).
- Redrawing the lettering cleaner: the vector follows the owner's picture, small irregularities included.

## Decisions
- **Inline vector shapes in the text colour**, not an image file: the logo then follows every theme, including high contrast, with no second file per theme.
- **The letters are outlines.** The lettering is custom (24 well-known fonts were compared and none matches), so there is no font to load.
- **The name in the logo is "Neruma"**, the owner's own design, while the text of the site still writes NERUMA (`CLAUDE.md` naming rule). Left as the owner designed it.

## Result
`src/components/brand.tsx` (the logo as two vector shapes; the folded sidebar hides the name), `src/app/globals.css` (sizes, 44 px on desktop and 36 px on a phone),
the three places that show it, `public/favicon.svg`, `public/brand/*.svg`. Tests: three new checks in `tests/platform.spec.ts` (light on dark and dark on light in
every theme, the name kept, the footer too) and the folded sidebar in `scripts/test-workspace.mjs`.
